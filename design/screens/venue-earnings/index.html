How payouts work

Plain language, no surprises.
When does the money arrive?
Once your bank is connected, your first payout sends right away. After that, money lands in your account two to three business days after each booking is marked done.
What does Aventure take?
Nothing. As a founding venue you pay zero commission. The full amount a crew pays is yours. We lock this in for your founding period.
Why connect a bank only now?
We do not ask for bank details during onboarding. We wait until you have a real payout ready, so the only time Stripe appears is when there is money to send you. Your details are held securely by Stripe, not by us.
